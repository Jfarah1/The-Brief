# Source pool

The agent starts here but is not limited to this list. Edit freely: add or remove outlets, or change a lean label.

## What counts as "mainstream"
Mainstream (counts against the 50% budget): US/UK national legacy newspapers (NYT, WSJ, Washington Post, USA Today, LA Times, Guardian, Times of London, FT), TV networks and their sites (CNN, ABC, CBS, NBC, MSNBC, Fox News, Fox Business, CNBC, BBC, Sky), wire services (AP, Reuters, AFP, Bloomberg), NPR/PBS, Politico, Axios, The Hill, and aggregators (Yahoo News/Finance, MSN).

Everything else counts as **non-mainstream**: international outlets, independent and Substack publications, trade press, think tanks, NGOs, primary sources (court filings, central-bank statements, Vatican documents), and state media. State media counts as non-mainstream but must always be labeled as state media.
